How to Label Tweets

Read the tweet and select the emotion that best describes the overall emotion expressed in the tweet.

Emotion Labels

Anger — The tweet expresses anger, frustration, or irritation.

Fear — The tweet expresses fear, worry, anxiety, or nervousness.

Joy — The tweet expresses happiness, excitement, amusement, or pleasure.

Love — The tweet expresses affection, caring, or romantic/familial love.

Sadness — The tweet expresses sadness, grief, disappointment, or loneliness.

Surprise — The tweet expresses surprise, shock, or unexpectedness.

Tips

  • Choose the emotion that is most strongly expressed.
  • Focus on the emotion expressed by the text, not your own reaction.
  • Try to be consistent when labeling similar tweets.
